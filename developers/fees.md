# 💰 Fees

AltStore is a free-to-distribute platform.

On AltStore Classic, you don't pay any fees whatsoever. On AltStore PAL, your apps are subject to Apple's Core Technology Commission.

{% hint style="info" %}
[**Apple's Core Technology Commission (CTC)**](https://developer.apple.com/support/apps-in-the-eu/#ctc)

Apple charges a 5% commission on digital goods and services for apps distributed on alternative marketplaces, so be sure to read documentation on [reporting requirements](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/making-payments-to-apple/reporting-tokens-and-transactions/) and [making payments to Apple](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/making-payments-to-apple/overview-of-making-payments-to-apple/).
{% endhint %}
